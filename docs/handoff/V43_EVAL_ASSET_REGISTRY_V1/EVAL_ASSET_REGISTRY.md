## Frozen Chengchuan Base16 authority snapshot

The Engineering Eval authority snapshot is
`docs/handoff/V43_PRE_E2E_GOVERNANCE_AND_MAPPING_READINESS_CLOSURE_V1/21_CHENGCHUAN_SOURCE_ROLE_MODEL_CORRECTED.json`.
It is frozen by `V43_CHENGCHUAN_BASE16_CONTENT_ROLE_FREEZE_V1` for all
Chengchuan Base16 (`COM-01` through `COM-16`) Eval/E2E consumers: 7
`REFERENCE_ONLY`, 9 `EVIDENCE_CANDIDATE`, and 1
`UNKNOWN_REVIEW_REQUIRED` boundary (COM-06). All records remain
`synthetic=true`, `authority=SYNTHETIC_EVAL_ONLY`, and
`production_authority=NONE`.

Consumers must load this snapshot and fail closed on identity mismatch. Legacy
`material_type`/`evidence_category` inference is forbidden for Chengchuan
Base16; the snapshot's content-audited material type and source role are the
authority. This registry change is configuration-only and performed no E2E,
Provider, embedding, database, Gold, Fact, Mapping, Claim, or Writer action.
